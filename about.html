Travel Tips and Recommendations!
  • Best Time to Visit: Dry Season, May - October, for wildlife viewing
  • Must-try local dishes: Mopane worms, Seswaa, and Botswana Biltong
  • Don't forget to bring a camera for unforgettable wildlife shots!
  • Bring appropriate clothing for both hot and cold weather conditions
