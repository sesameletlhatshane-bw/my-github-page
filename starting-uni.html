First-Year Survival Guide🎒

First semester survival checklist

  • Attend all orientation sessions and campus tours.
  • Find your lecture halls in advance to avoid late coming and being lost.
  • Join student clubs or organizations to meet new people and stay engaged.
  • Do not skip lectures, once you start there's no going back! And that's when most get left behind

Few Essentials one can to Bring to School

For On-Campus Students

  • Bedding and pillows
  • Laundry basket and detergent
  • Desk lamp and study supplies
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Comfortable clothing and sneakers

For Off-Campus Students

  • Laptop and charger
  • Notebook or digital planner
  • Backpack for daily commuting
  • Snacks for long lecture days
  • Portable power bank

First Week Reality

Your first week at university might feel overwhelming;new people, new places, and a completely different learning style. That’s normal. Take things one step at a time and remember that everyone around you is figuring things out too.
