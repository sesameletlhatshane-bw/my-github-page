Study smart, not hard

Learn effective study strategies to maximize your academic performance.

Time Management

Effective time management is crucial for academic success. Create a study schedule and stick to it.

Also attend tutorials to help cut down on study time.

Active Learning

Engage with the material actively by taking notes, asking questions, and participating in discussions

Use famous study techniques like the Feynman Technique or Pomodoro Technique to enhance your learning.

Tools that actually help

NotebookLM- It helps turn notes, documents and sources into organized summaries and explanations.

Quizlet- Perfect for creating digital flashcards and studying on the go.

Notion- A versatile tool for organizing thoughts, tasks, and projects in one place.
